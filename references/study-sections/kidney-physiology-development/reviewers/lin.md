## Research focus
Lin is a pediatric nephrologist-scientist who studies how the kidney tubule responds to and recovers from injury, and why some injuries progress to CKD. Her earlier program, at UTSW with Peter Igarashi, used lineage tracing to show that regenerating tubules derive from intrarenal epithelial cells, not bone marrow. It also linked primary cilia, planar cell polarity and AKI to cystogenesis. At Columbia she built a program on proximal tubular autophagy in ischemia-reperfusion and ureteral obstruction (including new RFP-EGFP-LC3 reporter mice). She identified FoxO3, stabilized by hypoxia through prolyl-hydroxylase inhibition and Hif-1alpha, as a protective tubular stress-response factor in the AKI-to-CKD transition. Her current focus is developmental-origins nephrology. She asks how congenitally low nephron endowment, as in preterm birth, causes maladaptive glomerular and tubular hypertrophy, greater susceptibility to neonatal nephrotoxic AKI (gentamicin), CKD, and salt-sensitive hypertension. She uses new Ret-based ureteric-bud models. She is also a clinical collaborator in large Columbia genetics consortia: CAKUT CNV and exome studies, VUR, congenital obstructive uropathy, APOL1, pediatric nephrotic syndrome GWAS and IgA vasculitis. She also contributes to clinical genomics implementation and nephrologist surveys.

## Model systems and methods
- Mouse models of low nephron endowment. Ret(flox-V805A) analog-sensitive allele inhibited with NA-PP1 during late gestation (E16.5-E18.5). Doxycycline-inducible ureteric-bud Ret deletion (Hoxb7-rtTA; TetO-Cre; "RetUB del"), giving a 30-70% reduction in nephron number.
- Nephron/glomerular counting by acid maceration (HCl digestion). Blinded morphometry of glomerular area and PT diameter in QuPath, analyzed with mixed-effects regression to handle repeated measures.
- Neonatal AKI with gentamicin (P3-P9). Ischemia-reperfusion injury. Unilateral ureteral obstruction (UUO). AKI-to-CKD transition models.
- Continuous ambulatory blood pressure monitoring before and after high-salt diet. Fractional sodium excretion. Juxtaglomerular renin.
- Both sexes are used routinely and sex differences are reported (2023 JCI Insight, 2026 J Hypertension). Her 2026 paper explicitly notes that sex stratification reduced statistical power.
- Autophagy tools: CAG-RFP-EGFP-LC3 reporter mice she generated. Autophagic-flux analysis with sequential inhibitors. Tubular-specific Hif-1alpha and FoxO3 deletion. Phospho-mutant FoxO3 in cultured renal epithelial cells.
- Lineage tracing with tubule-specific permanent EGFP reporters. Bone-marrow transplantation with Y-chromosome FISH. Earlier conditional mutants: Kif3a, Apc, tamoxifen-inducible kidney-specific Cre.
- RNAscope with IHC co-detection. EM. Confocal imaging. Cytokine readouts (MCP-1, CXCL10).
- Collaborative: 4-thiouracil nascent-RNA tagging (Rosa26-Uprt) for cell-specific acute injury responses (Barasch lab). Human genomics cohorts (CNV, exome, GWAS) through Gharavi/Sanna-Cherchi/Kiryluk consortia. Retrospective neonatal cohorts (PDA and AKI in preterm infants, KDIGO neonatal criteria). MIS-C AKI cohort.

## Representative publications
1. Lin F, Moran A, Igarashi P. Intrarenal cells, not bone marrow-derived cells, are the major source for regeneration in postischemic kidney. JCI 2005. PMID 16007252. Her defining lineage-tracing paper. It sets the high bar she applies to cell-origin and regeneration claims.
2. Patel V, Li L, ..., Somlo S, Lin F, Igarashi P. Acute kidney injury and aberrant planar cell polarity induce cyst formation in mice lacking renal cilia. Hum Mol Genet 2008. PMID 18263895. Inducible Kif3a deletion. Shows age dependence and that AKI triggers cysts through altered oriented cell division.
3. Li L, Wang ZV, Hill JA, Lin F. New autophagy reporter mice reveal dynamics of proximal tubular autophagy. JASN 2014. PMID 24179166. Built the RFP-EGFP-LC3 tool to separate autophagosome induction from blocked flux after IRI. This informs her skepticism of static LC3 readouts.
4. Li L, Zviti R, ..., Hill JA, Lin F. FoxO3 regulates kidney tubular autophagy following urinary tract obstruction. JBC 2017. PMID 28705935. FoxO3 increases autophagosome formation and maintains Atg protein levels in UUO.
5. Li L, Kang H, Zhang Q, D'Agati VD, Al-Awqati Q, Lin F. FoxO3 activation in hypoxic tubules prevents chronic kidney disease. JCI 2019. PMID 30912765. Hypoxia/PHD/Hif-1alpha stabilize FoxO3, and tubular FoxO3 deletion worsens the AKI-to-CKD transition.
6. Lin F (first author), ... Molecular regulation and function of FoxO3 in chronic kidney disease. Curr Opin Nephrol Hypertens 2020. PMID 32427691. Her framing review on hypoxia and alpha-KG-dependent regulation of FOXO3.
7. Good PI, Li L, ..., Costantini F, Lin F. Low nephron endowment increases susceptibility to renal stress and chronic kidney disease. JCI Insight 2023. PMID 36626229. Introduces the Ret-based low-nephron models, with hypertrophy leading to CKD and worse gentamicin AKI. Her current flagship.
8. Serrano Herrera I, ..., D'Agati VD, Lin F, Good PI. Low nephron endowment increases susceptibility to salt-induced elevation of blood pressure in mice. J Hypertens 2026. PMID 41648930. Ambulatory BP before and after high salt in RetUB-del mice, with sex-dimorphic responses and blunted renin suppression and FENa in females.
9. Shen TH, ..., Lin F, ..., Barasch J. Snapshots of nascent RNA reveal cell- and stimulus-specific responses to acute kidney injury. JCI Insight 2022. PMID 35230973. Shows her interest in early, cell-specific injury markers beyond serum creatinine.
10. Verbitsky M, ..., Lin F, ... (large Columbia-led consortium). The copy number variation landscape of congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract. Nat Genet 2019. PMID 30578417. CAKUT CNV burden in 2,824 cases. She contributes as a pediatric nephrology phenotyping/clinical collaborator.
11. Milo Rasouly H, ..., Lin F, ... Exome analysis links kidney malformations to developmental disorders and reveals causal genes. Nat Commun 2025. PMID 40774958. Rare damaging variants in 248 CAKUT trios and 1,742 singletons, with a 14.1% diagnostic yield. Shows her link between developmental biology and human CAKUT genetics.
12. Ahram DF, ..., Lin F, ... Rare single nucleotide and copy number variants and the etiology of congenital obstructive uropathy. JASN 2023. PMID 36995132. A 733-case COU genomic screen (10% diagnostic yield). Relevant to her UUO/obstruction work.
13. Verbitsky M, ..., Lin F, ... Copy number variant analysis and GWAS identify loci with large effect for vesicoureteral reflux. JASN 2021. PMID 33597122. A 1,737-patient VUR cohort.
14. Majed B, ..., Lin F, ... Patent ductus arteriosus is associated with acute kidney injury in the preterm infant. Pediatr Nephrol 2019. PMID 30706125. A clinical neonatal AKI cohort (≤28 weeks, KDIGO neonatal criteria) that links her bench low-nephron program to NICU practice.
15. Gupta Y, ..., Lin F, ... Strong protective effect of the APOL1 p.N264K variant against G2-associated FSGS and kidney disease. Nat Commun 2023. PMID 38036523, with follow-up JAMA Netw Open 2026 (PMID 41811315). Consortium genetics collaboration.

## Likely reviewing lens (inferred)
- **Will value:**
  - Quantitative, blinded morphometry with appropriate statistics. Her own papers use mixed-effects models for repeated measures per animal and blinded measurement.
  - Rigorous nephron counting (acid maceration or stereology) rather than surrogate estimates.
  - Both sexes, with sex-dimorphic results reported honestly, including power limits.
  - Models that mimic a defined human exposure, such as preterm nephron deficit or neonatal gentamicin, and clear clinical relevance to pediatric patients.
  - Lineage tracing or genetic labeling for any cell-of-origin or regeneration claim. Her 2005 JCI paper overturned bone-marrow-repair claims.
  - Measurements of flux rather than static markers for dynamic processes like autophagy.
- **Will scrutinize hardest:**
  - Autophagy claims based on LC3-II or puncta counts without flux assays (reporter or inhibitor clamping). She co-authored the autophagy assay guidelines (Autophagy 2016, PMID 26799652).
  - Claims about AKI or CKD based only on serum creatinine or BUN without tubular injury markers, histology and long-term follow-up.
  - Low nephron number models that confound nephron deficit with other insults (systemic drug effects, growth restriction). She deliberately built UB-restricted, timed Ret inhibition and deletion to isolate the nephron deficit.
  - Developmental-timing claims (induction windows, the end of nephrogenesis in mouse vs. human), Cre/rtTA leakiness and littermate control design.
  - Hypertension claims: she uses continuous ambulatory BP before and after salt loading and will discount tail-cuff-only data or single-timepoint measurements (inference).
  - Translational framing of neonatal or pediatric outcomes, such as KDIGO neonatal AKI definitions.
- **Where she is an outsider:** podocyte transcriptional biology and chromatin (she would defer to colleagues such as Kreidberg), ion channel electrophysiology, micropuncture hemodynamics, transplant immunology, and large-scale computational genomics method development. On genetics she is a phenotyping and clinical collaborator rather than a statistical-genetics lead.
- **Likely questions at the table:**
  - "How was nephron number measured, and is the reduction comparable to the human preterm range?"
  - "Is this autophagy induction or blocked flux?"
  - "Were both sexes studied and powered?"
  - "Does the model separate nephron deficit from the injury itself?"
  - "How long was follow-up to show CKD rather than incomplete recovery?"
  - "Is the cell of origin shown by lineage tracing?"
