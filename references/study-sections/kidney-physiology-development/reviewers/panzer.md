## Research focus
Panzer studies the immune mechanisms of chronic active antibody-mediated rejection (cAMR) and transplant glomerulopathy (TG) after kidney transplantation. She focuses on B cells, B-cell survival cytokines (BAFF/BLyS, APRIL), complement, and macrophages. Her lab pairs a rat kidney transplant model (including a CRISPR-generated B-cell-deficient Lewis strain) with patient cohorts that have biopsy-confirmed rejection. The goal is diagnostic, prognostic, and therapeutic strategies against late graft loss. A second line of work, from her fellowship with Joshua Thurman, covers natural IgM and complement activation in glomerular disease (FSGS/MCD, factor H-deficient C3 glomerulopathy models). She also co-authors clinical cohort studies at UW on lupus nephritis (renal arteriosclerosis and ASCVD risk, multidisciplinary LN clinic care), recurrent GN, BK and CMV infection after transplant, and outcomes in anti-GBM disease. Her UW profile lists clinical and research interests in glomerulonephritis, LN, FSGS, immune-complex GN, and C3 glomerulopathy.

## Model systems and methods
- Rat kidney transplantation: minor-mismatch Fischer-344 to Lewis (a cAMR/TG model), full-mismatch Brown Norway to Lewis (acute rejection), and transfusion sensitization before transplant
- CRISPR/Cas9 B-cell-deficient Lewis rats (IgM truncation)
- Mouse sensitization and kidney transplant models (C57BL/6 recipients sensitized with BALB/c cells); APRIL/BLyS blockade with TACI-Ig or BAFF-R-Ig
- Banff scoring by a blinded transplant pathologist; quantitative electron-microscopy measurement of glomerular basement membrane duplication; C4d and C3 immunostaining
- Flow cytometry of B-cell subsets (transitional, regulatory) and T-cell subsets (Treg, Tfh); DSA flow crossmatch; ELISpot; mixed lymphocyte reaction; CellTrace proliferation
- Intragraft cytokine RT-qPCR (IFNγ, IL-1β, MCP-1/CCL2, IL-6); multiplex circulating cytokines
- Conditionally immortalized mouse glomerular endothelial cells isolated with a CD31-independent method; IFNγ and anti-MHC antibody activation in vitro; imaging flow cytometry
- Human cohorts: single-center retrospective UW transplant and LN cohorts (Cox models, person-year incidence); a prospective observational ABMR treatment cohort (steroids/IVIG/rituximab); the All-MAP biomarker study (donor-derived cell-free DNA by AlloSure, AlloMap, NanoString on FFPE biopsies)
- Complement and natural IgM assays in human nephrotic syndrome plasma and biopsies (with Thurman/Trachtman)

## Representative publications
1. Panzer SE, Laskowski J, ..., Holers VM, Thurman JM. IgM exacerbates glomerular disease progression in complement-induced glomerulopathy. Kidney Int 2015. PMID 25945405. Fellowship origin of her interest in natural IgM, complement, and B cells in glomerular injury.
2. Panzer SE, Wilson NA, Verhoven BM, ..., Reese SR. Complete B cell deficiency reduces allograft inflammation and intragraft macrophages in a rat kidney transplant model. Transplantation 2018. PMID 29215459. Introduces the CRISPR B-cell-deficient rat; ties B cells to macrophage infiltration and Treg/IL-10 shifts.
3. Reese SR, Wilson NA, ..., Zhong W, Panzer SE. B-cell deficiency attenuates transplant glomerulopathy in a rat model of chronic active antibody-mediated rejection. Transplantation 2021. PMID 33273321. Her signature senior-author finding. EM-quantified GBM duplication; she acknowledges small samples and that TG was attenuated, not eliminated.
4. Panzer SE, Joachim E, Parajuli S, Zhong W, Astor BC, Djamali A. Glomerular C3 deposition is an independent risk factor for allograft failure in kidney transplant recipients with transplant glomerulopathy. Kidney Int Rep 2019. PMID 30993233. Human biopsy cohort (n=111) linking complement to TG outcomes; C4d and C1q were not prognostic.
5. Wilson NA, Dylewski J, ..., Blaine J, Panzer SE. An in vitro model of antibody-mediated injury to glomerular endothelial cells: upregulation of MHC class II and adhesion molecules. Transpl Immunol 2020. PMID 31887408. Glomerular endothelium as an antigen-presenting target.
6. Dylewski JF, Wilson N, ..., Panzer SE, Blaine J. Isolation, purification, and conditional immortalization of murine glomerular endothelial cells of microvascular phenotype. MethodsX 2020. PMID 32944514. Shows her attention to whether a cell line truly has microvascular (CD31-negative) phenotype.
7. Wilson NA, Bath NM, ..., Panzer SE, Redfield RR. APRIL/BLyS blockade reduces donor-specific antibodies in allosensitized mice. Transplantation 2019. PMID 30830041. B-cell survival cytokines as a desensitization target; DSA fell but rejection rates did not change.
8. Panzer SE. Anti-BAFF therapy: a new tool to target B cells in antibody-mediated rejection? Transplantation 2020 (editorial). PMID 31596742. Her stated therapeutic thesis.
9. Degner KR, Wilson NA, ..., Panzer SE, ..., Djamali A. Short-term immunopathological changes associated with pulse steroids/IVIG/rituximab therapy in late kidney allograft antibody mediated rejection. Kidney360 2020. PMID 34476406. Prospective human immunophenotyping; BAFF/APRIL rose after B-cell depletion.
10. Thurman JM, Panzer SE, Le Quintrec M. The role of complement in antibody mediated transplant rejection. Mol Immunol 2019. PMID 31195225. Review of complement assays and inhibitors in AMR.
11. Panzer SE. Macrophages in transplantation: a matter of plasticity, polarization, and diversity. Transplantation 2022. PMID 33908380. Single-author review on macrophage ontogeny and phenotypes in allograft injury.
12. Trachtman H, Laskowski J, ..., Panzer SE, ..., Thurman JM. Natural antibody and complement activation characterize patients with idiopathic nephrotic syndrome. Am J Physiol Renal Physiol 2021. PMID 34459222. Human FSGS/MCD evidence for IgM-driven classical pathway activation.
13. Qian T, Hernday SE, ..., Panzer SE, Shusta EV, Palecek SP. Directed differentiation of human pluripotent stem cells to podocytes under defined conditions. Sci Rep 2019. PMID 30808965. Bioengineering collaboration on an hPSC podocyte protocol (Wnt activation through nephron progenitors).
14. Garg S, Bartels CM, ..., Panzer SE. High burden of premature arteriosclerosis on renal biopsy results in incident lupus nephritis. Arthritis Care Res 2021. PMID 31909878. Senior author on a biopsy re-read (Banff arteriosclerosis) study in LN; follow-on work linked this to 10-year ASCVD (PMID 39700421).

## Likely reviewing lens (inferred)
- **Will value:** a preclinical model paired with a patient biopsy cohort, which is how her own program is built. Blinded pathology scoring (Banff) and quantitative histology over subjective grading (she measured GBM duplication by EM rather than relying on cg scores). Mechanistic links from immune cells to tissue lesions, plus a therapeutic angle (B-cell survival cytokines, complement inhibition).
- **Will scrutinize hardest:** claims that B-cell depletion or anti-CD20 "works". Her own papers stress conflicting trials, incomplete DSA removal, and the importance of timing, and she found BAFF/APRIL rising after rituximab. She will also look at the choice of rejection model (minor vs full mismatch, sensitization, cyclosporine run-in) and whether it truly reproduces chronic lesions. C4d as a marker gets skepticism: she notes it is transient in chronic settings and can come from the lectin pathway. In vitro endothelial work will draw questions about whether the cells have microvascular phenotype. She flags small rodent sample sizes in her own discussion and will likely ask about power. Macrophage studies that reduce cells to M1/M2 may draw objections.
- **Outsider topics:** nephron development, progenitor and transcription-factor biology, tubular transport physiology and electrophysiology, acid-base and electrolyte handling, PKD genetics, and organoids. On most applications at a "Kidney Physiology and Development" panel she would read as an informed generalist. She is most expert on glomerular injury, immune-mediated kidney disease, complement, and anything touching transplantation or glomerulonephritis.
- **Likely questions at the table:** "Is there human tissue or cohort validation of the mechanism?" "Does the injury model develop chronic lesions, or only acute changes?" "How was pathology scored, and was the scorer blinded?" "Are complement or immune readouts specific to the pathway claimed?" "Is the sample size adequate for an ordinal histology outcome?" "What is the translational path: which drug or biomarker?"
