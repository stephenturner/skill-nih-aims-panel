## Research focus
Irazabal is a physician-scientist in the Mayo PKD program. She is best known for the Mayo Imaging Classification (MIC) of ADPKD (htTKV-for-age classes 1A-1E plus atypical class 2), now widely used for prognostic enrichment and treatment selection. Her lab asks what biology precedes irreversible injury in early ADPKD, and how to measure it in young patients whose eGFR is still normal. Main themes: (1) oxidative stress and redox signaling, including NOX4 and the Nrf2 antioxidant response; (2) mitochondrial structure, dynamics, and function, and TCA-cycle and energetic dysregulation (metabolomics); (3) endothelial dysfunction and intrarenal microvascular rarefaction (homocysteine, ADMA, eNOS), now measured noninvasively with super-resolution ultrasound localization microscopy; (4) ADPKD as a temporally dynamic disease, with transcriptomic and miRNA profiles that change from early to late stages in Pkd1 RC/RC mice. Through colleagues Lilach Lerman and Alfonso Eirin she also co-authors on swine renovascular disease and scattered tubular-like cells.

## Model systems and methods
- Rodent PKD models: Pkd1 RC/RC (p.R3277C hypomorph) mice, including genetic-background effects (C57BL/6J, BALB/c, 129, F1 crosses) and a standardized preclinical testing protocol with MRI-based randomization; PCK rat; Pkd1/Prkar1a and Pde1a knockouts (co-author)
- Ultra-high-field small-animal MRI for in vivo TKV and longitudinal phenotyping; 3D micro-CT of renal microvasculature; histomorphometry, capillary density, perivascular fibrosis; electron microscopy of mitochondrial cristae
- Metabolomics (1H-NMR, LC-MS/MS) of kidney, plasma, and urine, with an optimized low-ischemia clamp-freezing kidney harvest; metabolite recovery from fixative
- mRNA-seq and miRNA-seq with integrated pathway and gene-metabolite network analyses, compared across PKD models and human ADPKD datasets
- Human cohorts: Mayo Translational PKD Center cohort, CRISP, HALT-PKD, TEMPO 3:4 (post hoc analyses); young early-stage ADPKD patients versus sex/age-matched controls
- Clinical imaging biomarkers: htTKV by stereology/ellipsoid, MIC, quantitative MRI (DWI, MT, BOLD, MRE), ultrasound localization microscopy (vessel density, tortuosity, reliability statistics)
- Oxidative-stress biomarkers (8-OHdG, F2-isoprostanes), Nrf2 targets, NOX4, urinary/plasma mtDNA copy number
- Statistical prediction modeling: longitudinal mixed models for eGFR decline, prognostic enrichment simulations

## Likely reviewing lens (inferred)
- Values: biomarkers and endpoints that work in early disease, before eGFR falls. Her work starts from the premise that TKV and eGFR change late or reflect only one process. Expect her to reward aims that measure the biology (vascular, metabolic, redox) directly and align it with disease stage.
- Values: rigor in preclinical PKD studies. She has co-published a standardized Pkd1 RC/RC protocol (genetic background control, MRI-based randomization, positive-control drug) and a paper showing that a short tissue-harvest delay produces false-positive and false-negative metabolomics results. She uses both sexes in rodent studies and reports reliability statistics (ICC, CV) for new human imaging measures.
- Scrutinizes: model choice and genetic background (rapid Pkd1-null versus slowly progressive hypomorphs; strain effects), the stage at which an intervention or measurement is made, and whether mouse timepoints map onto human disease classes.
- Scrutinizes: patient selection and enrichment in human studies (Mayo class 1A-1E versus atypical class 2), power for eGFR-slope outcomes, and confounding in cross-sectional biomarker studies.
- Scrutinizes: metabolomics and oxidative-stress claims without attention to sample handling, the ROS source (mitochondria versus NOX), or redox balance in both directions (oxidative versus reductive stress).
- Outsider areas: ion-channel biophysics and ciliary electrophysiology, nephron progenitor and developmental lineage biology, skeletal muscle physiology, pediatric-specific ARPKD biology. She would judge these on rigor, translational relevance, and premise.
- Likely questions: "At what disease stage, and in which model or patient class, is this measured?" "How will you show the biomarker adds information beyond htTKV and Mayo class?" "Is the mouse model's genetic background controlled, and are both sexes included?" "How were tissues harvested for metabolomics?" "Is the readout reproducible (test-retest, inter-observer)?"
