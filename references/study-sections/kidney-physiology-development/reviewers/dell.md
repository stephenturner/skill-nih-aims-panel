## Research focus
Dell is a pediatric nephrologist and clinical/translational investigator whose core program is autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD) and, more broadly, pediatric cystic kidney disease. Her early lab work (bpk mouse, PCK rat) examined the TGF-alpha/EGFR axis, TACE inhibition, and intrarenal renin-angiotensin activation in cystic kidney and congenital hepatic fibrosis. For more than 15 years, with MR physicist Chris Flask, she has developed quantitative, non-contrast MRI biomarkers (T2-weighted cyst burden, MR fingerprinting T1/T2, arterial spin labeling perfusion, MR elastography, diffusion) first in rodent ARPKD models and now in children and young adults, motivated by the problem that eGFR decline and total kidney volume are poor trial endpoints in ARPKD. She is also a long-standing contributor to multicenter pediatric CKD and nephrotic syndrome cohorts (CKiD, NEPTUNE) and pediatric trials (sparsentan DUPLEX pediatric subgroup, paricalcitol), plus ARPKD consensus and patient-focused drug development work.

## Model systems and methods
- Rodent ARPKD models: PCK rat (Pkhd1 ortholog; kidney and liver disease, octreotide treatment), bpk mouse (Bicc1; TGF-alpha null crosses), conditionally immortalized collecting-tubule principal cells from bpk x Immorto mice
- Preclinical high-field MRI (7 T): T2-weighted cyst volume/renal cystic burden, MR fingerprinting, ASL-FISP perfusion, DP-FISP diffusion, liver T1 relaxometry against histology/hydroxyproline
- Clinical quantitative MRI at 3 T: MRF T1/T2 mapping, ASL perfusion, MR elastography (kidney and liver stiffness), radiomics; repeatability and two-site reproducibility
- Measured GFR (iohexol clearance) as gold-standard comparator
- Prospective multicenter cohorts: CKiD (ARPKD subgroup analyses: GFR decline, growth, neurocognition), NEPTUNE (MCD/FSGS, transcriptomics, APOL1, PROs), MyKidneyHealth PRO cohort
- Pediatric RCTs (sparsentan vs irbesartan pediatric subgroup; paricalcitol phase 3) and single-center retrospective outcome studies (PUV eGFR slopes, SRNS on calcineurin inhibitors, pediatric transplant)
- qRT-PCR, immunohistochemistry, Western blotting in rodent tissue (earlier work)

## Likely reviewing lens (inferred)
- Values: endpoints and biomarkers that actually track progression in pediatric disease; her CKiD work showed eGFR slope and proteinuria are weak in ARPKD, and her preclinical MRI work showed cyst burden beats TKV. Expect her to reward aims that validate a readout against histology or a gold standard (iohexol GFR, hydroxyproline, cystic index) and that consider translation to children.
- Values rodent-to-patient continuity. Her own program pairs a rodent model readout with a human measurement; she will likely look for a stated path from mechanism to a measurable clinical correlate.
- Scrutinizes: claims that a pathway "drives" cystogenesis based only on expression or pharmacology. Her TGF-alpha-null cross showed a pathway can look essential and turn out dispensable. She will probably ask for genetic necessity/sufficiency tests and for consideration of redundant ligands/receptors (EGFR family).
- Scrutinizes: model choice for ARPKD versus ADPKD. She knows PCK rat and bpk mouse phenotypes well and will question whether a model recapitulates collecting-duct microcysts and liver fibrosis, and whether kidney size is an appropriate outcome (it is not in ARPKD at later stages).
- Scrutinizes: cilia claims. Her review states cilia modulate severity but are not required for cysts; she may push on whether a cilia-localization finding is causally linked to the phenotype.
- Scrutinizes: sample size and feasibility in rare pediatric disease; recruitment estimates; age and developmental stage as variables.
- Outsider areas: ion-channel biophysics, single-cell transport physiology, micropuncture, structural biology, and nephron developmental lineage tracing. She would read these as an informed generalist and weight significance and translational relevance.
- Likely questions: "What is the readout of progression and has it been validated against histology or measured GFR?" "Does this model reflect ARPKD (collecting duct, liver) or ADPKD?" "Is the pathway necessary, or only correlated?" "How would this translate to a pediatric trial endpoint?" "Are both sexes and relevant developmental ages included?"
